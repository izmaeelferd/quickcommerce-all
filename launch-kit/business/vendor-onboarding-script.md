## 1. Before the visit
- [ ] Check the restaurant is inside the launch zone (default delivery radius 10 km from the customer; launch zone: [ZONE]).

## 2. Pitch script (cold visit / cold call), about 3 minutes

**Opening (15 s)**
> "Hi, I'm [YOUR NAME] from FeastFlow. We're a new food-delivery app launching in [ZONE / CITY] on [DATE]. We're onboarding a small group of founding restaurants in this area, and [RESTAURANT NAME] is on our list because [genuine reason: popular dish / ratings / footfall]. Do you have 3 minutes?"

**Discovery questions (ask, then listen; note answers)**
1. "Do you do delivery today? Through which apps, or your own staff?"
2. "Roughly what share of your orders are delivery?"
3. "What's the biggest headache with delivery apps right now: commission, discounts, payouts, support, something else?"
4. "Who handles orders on the counter, and do they use a phone or a computer?"

**Value pitch: tailor to their pain**
> "Here's how FeastFlow works for you:
> - **You stay in control.** New orders arrive in the FeastFlow Partner app and on a web dashboard with a sound alert and an **accept timer**. You accept or reject. Then you mark *Preparing* and *Ready*, and our rider picks up.
> - **Your menu, your way.** Add items, categories, add-ons and photos, mark an item out of stock with one tap, set your opening timings, and close the shop with an open/close switch when you're too busy.
> - **Clear money.** Our commission is a flat [18% standard / LAUNCH OFFER] on the food value only. Packaging charges [₹15] are paid by the customer and **go to you in full**, with no commission on packaging. You can see every order's earning and your payout history in the app.
> - **Reviews you can answer.** Customers rate you and you can **reply** to reviews from the dashboard.
> - **We deliver.** Our riders handle delivery, with live tracking for the customer and an OTP handover, so the right person gets the food.
> - **Founding partner offer:** [X]% commission for your first [N] months, plus [featured placement / home-screen banner] at launch."

**Close**
> "Can we get you set up today? It takes about [20–30] minutes if you have your FSSAI licence, bank details and menu handy. You'll be live before our launch on [DATE]."

If they hesitate: *"Can I leave the offer sheet and come back on [DAY] at [TIME] to finish the setup?"* Always book a specific follow-up.

## 5. FAQ for restaurant owners (accurate to the current product)

**How do I receive orders?**
In the **FeastFlow Partner** app (Android/iOS) or the **Partner web dashboard** at [PARTNER DASHBOARD URL]. New orders arrive in real time with a notification and an accept timer.

**How long do I have to accept?**
[5] minutes (admin-configurable). If an order is not accepted in time it is automatically rejected and the customer is refunded.

**What statuses do I update?**
Accept → Preparing → Ready. The rider then marks Picked and Delivered (with the customer's 4-digit OTP).

**Can I manage my menu myself?**
Yes: items, categories, add-ons, prices, photos, veg/non-veg, and an **availability toggle** per item. Changes show in the customer app right away.

**Can I set my opening hours?**
Yes, under **Timings**, plus an **open/close** toggle for ad-hoc closures. Customers can also schedule orders into delivery slots ([Lunch 12–14, Snacks 16–18, Dinner 19–22]) when you're open.

**How is my earning calculated?**
Per order: **food value − any coupon discount − commission + packaging fee**. Commission is [X]% of (food value − discount). Example (illustrative, at 18%, no coupon): ₹400 food → ₹400 − ₹72 + ₹15 packaging = **₹343**. You see this for every order in **Payouts**.

**Who pays delivery fees and tips?**
The customer pays them; they go to FeastFlow and the rider. They are never deducted from your earning.

**Who collects GST on the food?**
The customer pays [5%] GST on the food value, shown separately on the bill. [GST POLICY: who files/remits as agreed with your CA; under Indian rules the e-commerce operator is generally responsible for GST on restaurant services ordered through it.] It is not taken from your earning.

**When do I get paid?**
Earnings are credited to your FeastFlow balance when each order is **delivered**. Payouts go to your bank account [WEEKLY on DAY] once the balance is at least **₹500**. Each payout shows a bank reference in your Payouts screen.

**What happens if a customer cancels?**
If they cancel before you've been paid out, the order isn't settled to you. [POLICY for food already prepared: e.g. if a customer cancels after the 5-minute window, a 10% cancellation charge is applied, and we share [X] with you if you'd started preparing.]

**Can I reply to reviews?**
Yes, from **Reviews** in the app or dashboard.

**Can I get more visibility?**
Founding partners get [featured placement / banner] at launch. After that, featured placement and home/offers banners can be booked with our team ([ADS RATE CARD]).

**Can I run my own offers?**
Restaurant-specific coupons are set up by our team on request ([POLICY on funding]).

**Not available yet (don't promise):** in-app chat, self-serve ads, subscription plans, loyalty points, dine-in, group ordering, WhatsApp ordering.

## 7. Onboarding steps (who does what in the product)

1. **Vendor creates an account**: in the **FeastFlow Partner** app or the partner web dashboard ([PARTNER DASHBOARD URL]), registering as a restaurant partner (name, phone/email, password or OTP).
2. **Vendor fills in the restaurant profile** (Settings): name, address and map location, cuisines, logo/cover, GSTIN, bank details, prep time. The restaurant is created with status **pending** and is not visible to customers.
3. **Field rep / vendor adds the menu**: Categories, items, prices, add-ons, photos, veg flag, availability. Set **Timings**.
4. **Admin verifies documents** (FSSAI validity, bank details, agreement signed).
5. **Admin approves**: **Admin Panel → Operations → Vendors** → approve the restaurant (status → approved). It goes live to customers in the delivery area.
6. **Admin sets commission (if launch offer)**: **Finance → Commission** → set a per-restaurant override (e.g. [10]%). Add the end date to the **commission tracker** (overrides do not expire automatically; revert to global on the date).
7. **Optional visibility**: **Operations → Restaurants** → toggle **Featured**; or create a **Banner** (Config → Banners) linking to the restaurant with start/end dates.
8. **Training (10 min)**: accept within the timer, update Preparing/Ready, toggle items off when sold out, open/close switch, reply to reviews, read Payouts.
9. **Test order**: place one real order with the owner watching; confirm notifications and alert sound on their device.
10. **Follow-up**: day 1 call, day 7 performance review (acceptance rate, prep time, ratings).

To pause or remove a restaurant: **Operations → Vendors / Restaurants** → suspend or deactivate.

## 8. Commission negotiation guide

**Standard:** [18]% of (food value − discount). Global setting in **Finance → Commission**.

**Your levers (all via the per-restaurant override):**
| Offer | When to use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Founding partner | First [N] restaurants in a zone | [10]% for [3] months, then [15]% for [3] months, then standard |
| Volume step-down | High-volume or anchor brands | [16]% above [N] orders/month (review monthly, set manually) |
| Exclusivity | Restaurant lists only on FeastFlow | −[2–3] points |
| Featured bundle | Restaurant wants visibility | Standard commission + [N] weeks featured free |
| Cuisine gap filler | You lack a cuisine in the zone | Founding rate even after the founding window |

**Guardrails**
- Floor: never below [X]% without founder approval. Check `unit-economics.md`: at 10% commission the illustrative contribution per order roughly halves (≈ ₹22 vs ≈ ₹54 at 18% for a ₹400, 4 km order).
- Every reduced rate has an **end date** in the agreement and in the tracker.
- Don't trade commission for vague promises ("we'll send lots of orders"). Tie step-downs to measurable volume.
- Don't negotiate packaging (it's global, ₹[15], fully to the restaurant) or delivery fees (customer-side).

**Commission tracker columns:** `Restaurant | Agreement date | Override % | Start | End | Revert-to % | Owner | Reverted? (Y/N)`

## 9. Coupon funding: say this clearly
In the current system, **any coupon discount on an order is deducted from the restaurant's earning** (commission is then charged on the discounted value). Before you start onboarding, the company must choose one policy and put it in the agreement:
- **A.** Platform reimburses platform-wide coupons (e.g. WELCOME50) through a monthly settlement statement. Recommended for trust.
- **B.** Restaurant funds coupons, in exchange for a lower commission.
- **C.** Only restaurant-specific coupons, created with the restaurant's written consent.

Policy chosen: **[POLICY]**. Field reps must explain it in the first meeting. Surprises here are the fastest way to lose a partner.
