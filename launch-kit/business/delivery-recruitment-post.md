# FeastFlow Rider: Recruitment Kit

> Templates to recruit delivery partners for the **FeastFlow Rider** app. Replace every `[BRACKET]`. ## 1. How rider earnings work (use this text anywhere)

**Per delivery:**
```
Earning = ₹[25] base  +  ₹[6] × trip distance (km)  +  100% of the customer's tip
```
- Distance is the distance from restaurant to customer that the app calculates for the order.
- **Tips:** customers can tip ₹10 / ₹20 / ₹30 / ₹50 or a custom amount, and **you keep 100%**.
- Earnings are added to your **FeastFlow Rider wallet** as soon as each order is marked delivered. You can see every trip's earning in the app's **Earnings** and **History** screens.
- **Payouts:** transferred to your bank account / UPI ID [PAYOUT SCHEDULE, e.g. every Monday] with a reference number.
- **Incentives:** [PEAK-HOUR / RAIN / WEEKLY TARGET BONUS, if any: describe exactly, or delete this line].

**Illustrative examples (default settings; not a guarantee):**
| Trip distance | Base | Distance pay | Tip (example) | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 km | ₹25 | ₹12 | ₹0 | **₹37** |
| 3 km | ₹25 | ₹18 | ₹10 | **₹53** |
| 4 km | ₹25 | ₹24 | ₹20 | **₹69** |
| 6 km | ₹25 | ₹36 | ₹0 | **₹61** |

**Illustrative day:** [N] deliveries × average [4] km → [N] × (₹25 + ₹24) = ₹[N × 49] + tips. *Example only: 15 deliveries at 4 km = ₹735 before tips.* Actual earnings depend on order volume, hours online and distance. Fuel, vehicle and phone costs are your own [unless your agreement says otherwise].

## 5. Rider FAQ

**Do I have to pay anything to join?**
No. [Delivery bag / T-shirt deposit policy, if any: state it clearly.]

**How do I get orders?**
Go online in the app. When a restaurant near you starts preparing an order, it's offered to online riders nearby automatically. Accept it and you'll see the pickup and drop locations with navigation.

**How do I prove delivery?**
Ask the customer for the **4-digit OTP** shown in their app and enter it. You may also need to take a **photo** at drop-off.

**How much do I earn per order?**
₹[25] + ₹[6] × km + 100% of any tip. The app shows the payout for each order and your totals in **Earnings**.

**When do I get paid?**
Earnings are added to your in-app wallet when each order is delivered. We transfer to your bank/UPI [PAYOUT SCHEDULE] and you'll see the reference.

**What about cash-on-delivery orders?**
The app shows the exact amount to collect (cash orders are limited to ₹[2,000]). Deposit collected cash [COD CASH DEPOSIT PROCESS: when, where, how; e.g. daily at the hub or via UPI to the company account]. Cash is reconciled against your delivered COD orders [DAILY].

**Can I choose my working hours?**
Yes. Go online and offline whenever you like. Lunch and dinner peaks have the most orders.

**Why does the app need my location while I'm online?**
While you're online, your location is used to offer you nearby orders and to show the customer live tracking, even if the app is in the background (your phone shows a "You are online" notification on Android while it is shared). Go offline to stop sharing.

**What if the customer isn't reachable or the restaurant is late?**
Use the customer contact details shown on the order, or contact rider support at [RIDER SUPPORT PHONE]. Don't leave the order without instructions from support.

**What if I have an accident?**
Get to safety first, then call [EMERGENCY NUMBER] and rider support. [INSURANCE COVER DETAILS, if provided.]

**Is there a rating?**
Customers can rate the delivery. Consistently low ratings or misconduct can lead to suspension under the Rider Agreement.

**How long does approval take?**
Usually [X] working days after you upload complete documents. Admin reviews and approves riders in the admin panel; you'll be able to go online once approved.
