# Screenshot copy: FeastFlow, FeastFlow Partner, FeastFlow Rider

Conversion-optimised copy for all 8 screenshot slots of each app, used by both the Play Store and App Store listings.

## General rules (all apps)
- **Headline ≤ 30 characters** (counted below), one idea per screenshot, benefit first, verb-led where possible.
- **Subtext ≤ 60 characters**, explains the headline in plain words.
- First 3 screenshots do most of the work (they show in search results). Put the core promise in #1, the key differentiator in #2-3.
- Use a consistent template: headline top 20% of the canvas, device frame below, brand colour background (from Admin Panel → Config → Theme).
- Capture screens from a production-like build with clean demo data: real-looking restaurant names you have rights to, real dish photos, no "Lorem ipsum", no test@ emails, status bar at 9:41 with full battery/signal.
- All prices/earnings shown are demo values; never show ratings, user counts or "#1" claims you cannot prove.
- Localise: produce Hindi variants for India (and Arabic with RTL mirrored layout if you target the Gulf). The app already ships en, hi, es, ar, fr.
- Sizes: Google Play phone 1080×1920 (9:16) minimum; Apple 6.9" 1320×2868. Export PNG, no transparency, sRGB.

## A/B test ideas (Play Console → Store listing experiments)
- Customer #1: "Local favourites, delivered" vs "Food you love, tracked live".
- Customer order: live tracking at #2 vs search at #2.
- Rider #1: earnings-led ("Keep 100% of your tips") vs flexibility-led ("Go online. Start earning.").
- Partner #1: speed-led vs growth-led ("Grow your restaurant with FeastFlow").
Run one variable at a time, for at least 7 days, 50/50 split.
