# FeastFlow — Social Media Launch Kit

40 ready-to-post launch posts (Instagram, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook) with copy, visual direction and hashtag sets, plus brand voice notes and a posting calendar for the pre-launch → launch → first month window.

**Placeholders to replace everywhere:** `[CITY]`, `[APP LINK]`, `[WAITLIST LINK]`, `[PARTNER SIGNUP URL]`, `[RIDER SIGNUP URL]`, `[@HANDLE]`, `[LAUNCH DATE]`, `[FOUNDER NAME]`, `[RESTAURANT NAME]`, `[LOCALITY]`. Use `[@HANDLE]` for FeastFlow's own handle on each network.

**Truth rules (non-negotiable):**
- Every offer here matches the seeded coupons and default Business Rules: **WELCOME50** (50% off, max ₹150, min order ₹199, first order only, 1 per user), **FLAT100** (₹100 off above ₹599, 3 uses per user), **FREEDEL** (free delivery above ₹249, 5 uses per user), free delivery on every order above ₹499, referral ₹50 + ₹50 wallet credit, riders keep 100% of tips. If you change any value in the Admin Panel, edit the scheduled posts.
- No invented numbers: no user counts, ratings, "fastest", delivery-time promises, or customer quotes. Use real numbers only once you have them from Admin → Dashboard / Reports, and use placeholders like `[X] restaurants live` until then.
- Do not mention features that do not exist: no in-app chat (the app has a *call the restaurant* button), no loyalty points, no group ordering, no meal subscriptions, no dine-in, no AI search, one city only.

## 1. Brand voice

| | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| **Personality** | Warm, quick, local, quietly confident. Like a friend who knows every good kitchen in [CITY]. | Hype-heavy, shouty, "revolutionary", "disrupting". |
| **Sentences** | Short. Concrete. One idea per line. | Long paragraphs, jargon ("hyperlocal synergy"). |
| **Claims** | Specific, verifiable features: live tracking, delivery OTP, UPI/COD, slots, veg filter. | "Fastest delivery in town", "No. 1", "loved by thousands". |
| **Offers** | Code + value + condition in one breath: "WELCOME50: 50% off your first order, up to ₹150, min ₹199." | "Huge discounts!!!" without the cap and minimum. |
| **Restaurants** | Heroes. Name them (with permission), show their food and people. | Treat them as inventory. |
| **Riders** | Respect. Mention tips go 100% to them. Never joke about speed or traffic risk. | "Our riders race to you" or anything implying unsafe riding. |
| **Language** | English first; Hindi / Hinglish versions welcome (the app itself supports en, hi, es, ar, fr). | Mixing scripts in one line in a way that breaks readability. |
| **Emoji** | Max 1–2 per post on Instagram/Facebook, none in LinkedIn headlines. Food emoji, not faces. | Emoji walls. |

**Tagline options:** "Good food, in flow." · "Your city's kitchens, at your door." · "Order. Track. Feast."
**Name usage:** always "FeastFlow" (one word, capital F twice). Partner app = "FeastFlow Partner", rider app = "FeastFlow Rider".
**Visual identity:** use the colours and logo set in Admin → Config → Theme / Branding so app screenshots and social posts match. Real food photography from launch restaurants (with written permission) beats stock. Show the app UI (live map, offer card, slot picker) in at least every third post.

## 7. Community management

- Reply to comments within 2 hours during 11:00–23:00 in launch week.
- Order complaints → move to DM, ask for the order number (shown in the app), and resolve via Admin → Operations → Orders (refund if appropriate). Never post order details publicly.
- Do not argue about competitor pricing. Do not promise delivery times.
- Keep a saved-replies doc for: coupon not applying (check min order, first-order-only, per-user limit, expiry), refund timelines ([REFUND TIMELINE] — from your refund policy in Admin → Legal Pages), area not served (single delivery radius), partner/rider enquiries (links above).

## 8. Adapting for other regions
Change ₹ amounts to the local currency (Business Rules → currency), replace UPI/COD references with the payment methods enabled in Business Rules → paymentMethods, swap festivals and meal times, and translate copy (the app ships English, Hindi, Spanish, Arabic (RTL) and French UI strings, editable in Admin → Languages).
