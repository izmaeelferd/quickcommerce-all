## 6. Press do's and don'ts
- Give journalists a **dedicated test account** (not seeded demo accounts) and a test coupon with `usageLimit` set.
- Do not provide unverified numbers; "we'll share numbers after our first 90 days" is a fine answer.
- If asked about features not built (chat, loyalty, other cities), say "not today" — do not pre-announce dates you can't commit to.
- Approvals: every quote, figure and partner name signed off by [APPROVER] before sending.
