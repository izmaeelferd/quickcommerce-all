3. Allergens and dietary requirements

  • The veg-only filter relies on information supplied by restaurants and may not be error-free.
  • Special instructions you add to an order (e.g. "no peanuts") are passed to the restaurant, but we cannot guarantee they will be followed.
  • If you have a food allergy, intolerance or medical dietary requirement, contact the restaurant directly before ordering (use the call-restaurant button on the restaurant page) and do not eat food you are unsure about. 5. Estimated delivery times are estimates

Delivery times shown in the app are estimates, calculated from the restaurant's average preparation time, a buffer (configured value businessRules.etaBufferMinutes = 10 minutes) and distance (configured value businessRules.etaMinutesPerKm = 3 minutes per km). Actual times can vary due to restaurant workload, traffic, weather, rider availability, road closures and other factors. Scheduled-order slots (e.g. Lunch 12:00–14:00 — configured value businessRules.deliverySlots) are target windows, not guarantees. Late delivery is not a breach of contract, although we may offer goodwill credit at our discretion.
